# The home-network route. Started against XenithOS Cloud's public address, XenithOS asks for its
# address on its own network (/v1/lan), checks it answers there as the same server, and uses it
# from then on (route.txt). Then the Store, in one request, and Settings says "Connected at home".
#   XSIM_KIND=pc sim/run.sh sim/scripts/store_route.txt store_route <public a.b.c.d:port>
wait 200
shot route_home
press right
press a
wait 60
shot route_store
press b
wait 30
press right
press right
press right
press a
wait 40
shot route_settings
quit
